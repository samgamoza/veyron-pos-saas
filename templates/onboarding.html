What you can do on demo

  • Use Admin to add categories, brands, units, and products.
  • Open POS to ring up sales, discounts, and print receipts.
  • Use Inventory for stock movements and counts.
  • Your storefront is available on your tenant subdomain (see Owner → branding after onboarding).

Demo caps: up to {{ demo_max_products }} products and {{ demo_max_sales_month }} completed sales per calendar month. Upgrade anytime on the Plans page.

Recommended order

  1. Admin → Categories / Brands / Units — create at least one of each so products validate.
  2. Admin → Products — add a few items with price and stock.
  3. POS — optionally open a cash shift, then run a test sale.
  4. Owner dashboard — review alerts, branding, and hardware notes.
  5. Plans — when you are ready, pick a monthly package for full limits and production use.

When you purchase a plan

You keep the same workspace: products, sales history, and settings stay put. We remove demo caps, set your plan name and monthly subscription fee, and mark the account active. Subscription billing in the Philippines is usually handled via bank transfer, GCash/PayMaya, or PayPal — configure what you use under Owner settings when you go live.
